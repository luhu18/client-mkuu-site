About PlaceHolder

PlaceHolder started in 2019 with a small roaster and a simple idea: everyone deserves a place to slow down over a good cup. We wanted coffee that is fresh, fairly priced and made by people who care about it.

Today our team of eight roasts, brews and bakes in one room, and most of our regulars know each other by first name.

Roasted every week

Beans are roasted in small batches and packed within 48 hours, never left sitting on a shelf.

Sourced from farmers

We buy straight from growers and pay above the market price for the best harvests.

Open every day

Walk in any day from 7:00 to 19:00. Free Wi-Fi and plenty of seats for work or conversation.
