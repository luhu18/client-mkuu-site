What PlaceHolder offers

From a single cup to a full event, here is how we can serve you. Prices are in Kenyan shillings and may change with the season.

Fresh-roasted beans

Choose from five single-origin coffees and two house blends, ground to suit your brewer or left whole.

From KSh 650 per 250 g bag. Order in the shop or ask us to hold a bag for pickup.

Café & breakfast

Espresso drinks, filter coffee and cold brew, with fresh mandazi, sandwiches and a daily breakfast plate.

Drinks from KSh 180. Breakfast served from 7:00 to 11:30.

Catering & events

Coffee and snacks for meetings, weddings and community gatherings of 20 to 200 guests.

Brewing classes

A two-hour Saturday session on home brewing. Small groups, all equipment provided.

Gift boxes

Beans, a mug and a handwritten note, packed and ready to give. Delivery available in town.

Planning something special?

Tell us the date, the number of guests and what you have in mind, and PlaceHolder will send you a quote within one working day.
